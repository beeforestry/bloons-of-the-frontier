🎈 Bloons of the Frontier

Primates, Flora and the UNSC Frontier hold the line against bloons and the bug hive. Build towers, deploy troops, buff them with Cover, farm Credits and keep your farms organized.

Left-drag box-selects troops (Shift-click queues waypoints, P patrols, G formation, 1–4 abilities, F FPS) · right-drag / WASD / screen edges pan · wheel or pinch zooms · minimap jumps · click a glowing pad by your HQ to build Supply, Reactors and Barracks · ☄ MAC Blast and ✚ Heal Drop use Energy · Space starts a wave · keep the HQ alive
